## 🧠 Why I Do This Work

I’ve always been captivated by how the brain constructs our experience of the world. Our sensory systems aren’t passive cameras; they are active prediction engines, constantly negotiating with our environment, our memories, and our fears.

When you step into Virtual Reality (XR), you aren’t just looking at software: you’re speaking directly to those prediction loops. Immersion and embodied cognition give us the ability to safely re-frame how the nervous system responds to anxiety and trauma in ways a flat glass screen never could.

This isn’t just theoretical for me. My perspective is deeply informed by **living with Multiple Sclerosis (MS)**. When your own central nervous system behaves unpredictably, neuroscience stops being merely an academic discipline. It becomes personal. It teaches you deep respect for neural plasticity, an intimate understanding of vulnerability, and an unwavering commitment to building assistive technology that is ethical, humane, and rooted in lived reality.

## 🗺️ The Concept Behind This Portfolio

Most modern tech portfolios look like software dashboards: sterile and cold.

I wanted this site to feel like a **cartographer’s field desk** or an explorer's expedition journal. Mapping the human mind, understanding chronic illness, and designing clinical interventions are messy, uncharted frontiers. They require patience, craft, and attention to detail.

Here is how the narrative chapters unfold:

* **Chapter I · Summary & Manifesto**: An illuminated parchment dossier outlining my research ethos, bridging psychiatric research, behavioral science, and purposeful technology.
* **Chapter II · Chronicle of Experience**: A grounded look at my professional journey. Before diving deep into academic psychiatry, I spent years wrangling data pipelines, architecting cloud solutions on GCP, running analytics, and coaching individuals navigating careers with chronic illness.
* **Chapter III · The Signpost (Education & Background)**: An interactive signpost (complete with wind-deflecting canopy foliage) tracing my academic path — from cognitive neuroscience and psychology at the University of the Witwatersrand to ongoing psychiatric research at Stellenbosch University.
* **Chapter IV · Collaborate (The Interactive Brief)**: Rather than an impersonal contact form, this is a guided 7-stage conversation. It asks real questions about product concept, patient cohorts, clinical promise, and desired outcomes. Visitors can attach documents and compile a clean project brief ready to export or print.
* **Chapter V · The Dispatch (Contact)**: Direct lines to reach me, grab my email in one click, or connect on LinkedIn.

## 🍃 Craft, Aesthetics & Interaction

Software should feel alive. A few personal touches baked into the experience:

* **Living Topographic Parchment**: A canvas of organic terrain elevation contours, ink washes, and cartographic coordinate markings that adapt between warm daytime parchment and midnight ink.
* **Zhukovsky Leaf Dynamics**: Transitions between portal cards compute aerodynamic thin-plate fluttering keyframes, mimicking the gentle drift of dry leaves.
* **Interactive Timber Canopy**: The wooden signpost in the Education chapter reacts to your cursor — foliage gently leans with the breeze and rustles on touch.
* **Overhead Festoon Lights**: Warm string lights hung across the top of the canvas, offering a subtle ambient glow.
* **Directory Command Palette**: Press `⌘K` or `Ctrl+K` anytime to pull up an index drawer and jump straight to any chapter or setting.
* **Day / Night Moods**: Press `T` to toggle between **Daybreak** and **Nocturne**.
